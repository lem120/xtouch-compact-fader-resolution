X-Touch Compact Anchored Same-MSB Hysteresis Test R10

Uses external hard anchors only to establish a reproducible starting state, then performs the decisive target comparison entirely inside one MSB bucket.

Non-destructive MIDI test. Sends ordinary Pitch Bend only. No SysEx and no firmware writes. Close Ableton Live, X-TOUCH Editor and other MIDI applications. Put the Compact in Mackie Control mode. Do not touch the fader while the sequence is running.

Why R10 exists

R9 showed that a same-MSB LOW↔HIGH span of 127 raw counts is not always sufficient to reset the servo state. At some regions, the fader moved LOW→HIGH but ignored the requested HIGH→LOW return. R10 therefore establishes each trial from a deliberately distant anchor before settling onto the local LOW or HIGH reference.

Local bucket for MSB M:
LOW  = M << 7
HIGH = LOW + 127

Hard LOW anchor  = (M - 2) << 7
Hard HIGH anchor = ((M + 2) << 7) + 127

The hard anchor is used only to establish direction/state.
The decisive targets remain:
LOW + 112 and LOW + 113
with MSB unchanged at M.
